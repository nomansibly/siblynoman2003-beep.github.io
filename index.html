02 / A small engineering experiment

More heat.
How much more airflow?

Try a simple steady-state energy balance. Change the heat load or airflow and see the air temperature rise respond.

60 kW
6.0 m³/s
Predicted air temperature rise
8.29 °C
AIR INHEAT LOADAIR OUT

Doubling airflow halves the temperature rise at a fixed heat load.

ΔT = Q / (ρ × V̇ × cₚ)
Assumes all heat transfers to air, ρ = 1.2 kg/m³ and cₚ = 1005 J/(kg·K). No humidity, heat storage, pressure loss or spatial gradients. An educational calculation, not equipment sizing advice.
